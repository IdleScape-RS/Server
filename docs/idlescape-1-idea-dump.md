Okay, this is going to be basically an idea dump of Idlescape, which is going to be a 2004 private server. 2004, I mean Lost City based private server — I don't care what year it is. The important thing that I want all of Idlescape's developments to be based on is: it's like an idle game where you AFK and you get upgrades, and there's high scores where you can compete. It's like an idle game. Idle game plus RuneScape.

## Client changes

First of all, the login: I don't want it to be username and password. When you get to the main screen, it shouldn't say "new user" or "existing user". It should just have one button that says "login with Discord", and then it should be a Discord login.

After you log in: instead of being in Tutorial Island with a tutorial running, you should be in Tutorial Island, but there's no tutorial. You're in the Tutorial Island building, and you can exit the building, and then you get into this area that has the fishing, the woodcutting, the firemaking — and then usually there's a fence that you go through to progress to where you make bread for the first time. So that fence is blocked off. We're only going to be interacting with this very small area of Tutorial Island to get started.

The welcome tutor: remove their dialogue. Remove the dialogue of the person who gives you the fishing net as well, and replace it. The welcome tutor should just say "Welcome to Idlescape", and the person who gives you the fishing net would just be like — I don't know, we'll figure out the text later.

So basically you're going to be in the starting area of the spawn house. The only content I think there's going to be: upstairs in the spawn house, there should be a bank booth, so you can access your bank.

The Tutorial Island tutor — there is a "talk to" option, it just says "welcome to Idlescape, a game that you progress by not playing" or "welcome to Idlescape, a game you progress by leaving it open in your browser". And then it has a trade option, and so you can trade him.

He gives you... let's just say he sells an iron axe for 20k. And then he buys logs for 1 gp each. And then you start with a bronze axe — he sells a bronze axe for 0 gp. So you can take the bronze axe and go outside.

## Woodcutting

I think there's an oak tree, and there's some normal trees. I want you to edit the trees so that trees don't despawn, you just chop them forever.

And when you get logs — right now, logs in RuneScape don't stack. I want them to stack. So you get logs and they stack, because then you click on the tree...

Also there's no logout timer, so remove the logout timer. Now if you click on a tree, you're going to start chopping it, the tree's never going to disappear, and you're never going to get a full inventory.

Also disable random events, so there's no random events. Your inventory's just going to have 10,000 logs, 20,000 logs, 30,000 logs, and you'll never run out of logs or just stop. Then you can go to the store and sell them.

## Shops

When I think about selling and buying — right now there's "sell 1", "sell 5", "sell 10". I want to add a "sell x" option. Some of the skills have "buy x", or "fletch x" — so there should be "sell x", and that enables the player to sell large amounts in bulk without needing to spam-click. So now I can sell 30,000 logs in one go, and this is going to be fundamental for the game progression, because nobody's going to want to spam-click selling.

## Firemaking

For firemaking: you can use a tinderbox on some logs and start making a fire. I guess the guide should also give out a tinderbox for free — actually no, tinderboxes should cost 100k, because why not. So the tinderbox costs 100k.

If there's a fire, you should be able to use a log on the fire, and that gives you just as much XP as burning a fire normally. And then it should loop — it should just keep putting the log on the fire. And as long as you put a log on the fire, it resets the fire's burning countdown, so the fire is not going to disappear as long as people are putting logs into the fire.

The really interesting mechanic here is that a tinderbox costs 100k, so if somebody else lights a fire, you can just use their fire, and that's fine.

## Progression gate

The fence to get to the next area — you should only be able to get to the next area if you have, let's say, 300 total level. So we should just make it say "you cannot go here until you're past 300 total level". And by the time anybody gets past 300 total level, I'll update it. So we can just have that text there for now — the check doesn't actually do anything, it's just if you click the fence, that's what it says.
